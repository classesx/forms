Frequently asked questions

What are Punjab arms forms?

Arms forms are the application forms used for arms license services in Punjab, such as new arms license, renewal, entry, addition or deletion of a weapon, NOC for sale of weapon, duplicate license and police verification.

Where can I download arms forms in PDF?

Every form on this site has a Preview button and a Download PDF button. Many forms are also available as editable DOCX files that you can fill on a computer or phone.

Is this the official government website?

No. This is a free, unofficial Sewa portal made for public convenience. Please verify the latest form on the official Punjab Government e-Forms portal before submission.

Is it free? Are there any ads?

Yes, it is completely free. There are no ads, no sign-up and no fees.

How do I search for a form?

Type the form type in the search box, for example NOC, entry, renewal, deletion or verification, or use the category buttons. Press the / key to jump to search quickly.
